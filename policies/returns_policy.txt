## Voltcart Returns & Refunds Policy

Voltcart is a fictional online electronics store used as the demo tenant for ShopDesk AI.
Replace this file with your own store's policy before going live.

### Who can return an item
- Any item from an order that is **SHIPPED** or **DELIVERED** can be returned.
- Orders that are still **PLACED** (not yet dispatched) cannot be returned. Cancel them instead; see the cancellation FAQ.
- **CANCELLED** orders have already been refunded and cannot be returned.

### Return window
- You have **30 days from the date of delivery** to ask for a return.
- After 30 days we can only accept a return where consumer protection law requires us to, for example a manufacturing defect covered by warranty.

### Condition of the item
- Unused items must come back with the original box, accessories, manuals and free gifts.
- Items that are faulty, damaged in transit or not what you ordered can be returned in any condition. Please describe the problem when you raise the request.
- We cannot accept returns of items that were physically damaged by the customer, liquid-damaged, or opened from sealed hygiene packaging (for example in-ear tips) unless they are faulty.

### How to request a return
1. Tell the support assistant the order number, the product you want to return and the reason.
2. The assistant checks that the order is yours and that the item is eligible.
3. A Voltcart team member reviews every return request before it is accepted. The assistant will never confirm a return on its own.
4. You receive an email when the request is received and another when it is approved or rejected.

### Review time
- Most return requests are reviewed within **2 to 5 business days**.

### Return shipping
- **Faulty, damaged or wrong item:** Voltcart arranges and pays for the pickup.
- **Change of mind:** a return pickup fee of **INR 99** is deducted from the refund.
- Please hand the parcel over to the courier within 7 days of approval, or the approval may lapse.

### Refunds
- Refunds go back to the original payment method (UPI, card or net banking).
- Once the item reaches our warehouse and passes inspection, the refund is issued within **5 to 10 business days**. Your bank may take a few extra days to show it.
- For a return of one item from a multi-item order, only that item's price is refunded.
- Original shipping charges are refunded only when the return is due to our error or a faulty product.

### Exchanges
- We do not swap items directly. Return the item and place a new order for the replacement.

### Rejected returns
- If an item fails inspection or the request is outside the policy, we explain the reason by email and can ship the item back to you.
