## Voltcart FAQ: Cancellations, Returns and Refunds

Voltcart is a fictional online electronics store used as the demo tenant for ShopDesk AI.
Replace this file with your own store's FAQ before going live.

### Cancellations

**Can I cancel my order?**
Yes, as long as its status is **PLACED**, meaning it has not left our warehouse yet. Ask the support assistant to cancel it and give the order number.

**Is the cancellation instant?**
No. A Voltcart team member approves each cancellation request, usually within a few hours on business days. You get an email when it is done.

**When do I get my money back after a cancellation?**
The full amount is refunded to the original payment method as soon as the cancellation is approved. Banks usually show it within 3 to 7 business days.

**My order already shipped. Can I still cancel?**
Not once it is **SHIPPED** or **DELIVERED**. You can refuse the delivery, or wait for it to arrive and request a return within 30 days of delivery.

**Can I cancel just one item from my order?**
Cancellation applies to the whole order. To keep some items, cancel and reorder, or wait for delivery and return the items you do not want.

### Returns

**How long do I have to return something?**
30 days from the delivery date.

**How do I start a return?**
Tell the support assistant the order number, the product and why you are returning it. It checks eligibility and sends the request for review.

**Why can't the assistant approve my return straight away?**
Every return is checked by a person to prevent mistakes and fraud. Most are reviewed within 2 to 5 business days.

**Can I return an order that has not shipped yet?**
No. Orders in **PLACED** status should be cancelled instead.

**Do I pay for the return pickup?**
Only for change-of-mind returns (INR 99, deducted from the refund). Pickups for faulty, damaged or wrong items are free.

### Refunds

**How long does a refund for a return take?**
5 to 10 business days after the item passes inspection at our warehouse.

**Where does the refund go?**
To the original payment method. We cannot refund to a different account or card.

**I was refunded less than I paid. Why?**
For a partial return only the returned item is refunded, and the INR 99 pickup fee applies to change-of-mind returns. Shipping charges are refunded only if the return was our fault.

### Account and privacy

**Can the assistant see other customers' orders?**
No. It only looks up orders that belong to the email address you are signed in with.

**How do I reach a human?**
Ask the assistant to raise a support ticket. A team member will reply by email.
