## Voltcart Shipping Policy

Voltcart is a fictional online electronics store used as the demo tenant for ShopDesk AI.
Replace this file with your own store's policy before going live.

### Where we deliver
- We deliver to all serviceable PIN codes in India.
- We do not ship internationally at the moment.
- A few remote PIN codes may only be eligible for standard delivery.

### Order processing
- Orders are packed and handed to the courier within **1 to 2 business days** of payment.
- Orders placed after 6 pm or on Sundays and public holidays start processing on the next business day.
- While an order is waiting to be dispatched its status is **PLACED**. Once the courier collects it the status changes to **SHIPPED**, and to **DELIVERED** after the courier confirms delivery.

### Delivery options and times
| Option | Typical delivery after dispatch | Fee |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 3 to 7 business days | Free on orders of INR 499 or more, otherwise INR 49 |
| Express | 1 to 3 business days | INR 149 |
| Same-day (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru only) | Same day for orders paid before 12 noon | INR 249 |

Delivery times are estimates. Weather, strikes, festivals and courier capacity can cause delays.

### Tracking
- You receive a tracking link by email and SMS when the order ships.
- The support assistant can tell you the current status of any of your orders.

### Late or missing parcels
- If a standard parcel has not arrived **3 business days after the estimated date**, contact support and we will raise a trace with the courier.
- If the courier confirms the parcel is lost, we send a replacement or a full refund, whichever you prefer.
- If the tracking shows "delivered" but you did not receive it, tell us within **48 hours** so we can investigate.

### Damaged parcels
- Refuse a parcel that is visibly damaged or tampered with, or record an unboxing video and report it within 48 hours of delivery.

### Changing the address
- The address can only be changed while the order is still **PLACED**. After dispatch it cannot be changed; you may refuse the delivery and place a new order instead.

### Cash on delivery
- Cash on delivery is not offered. Pay by UPI, card or net banking.
